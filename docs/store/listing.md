## Full description (max 4000 characters)

```text
Sadho is a devotional alarm clock, timer and calendar for your daily practice. Everything stays on your phone: no account, no ads, no tracking.

ALARM CLOCK THAT FOLLOWS THE SUN
• Sun-based alarm: wake at sunrise or sunset, or at a set time before or after it (for example 45 minutes before sunrise for Amritvela). The time moves with the sun every day.
• Rings like a real alarm clock: exact to the minute, on the alarm volume, full screen over the lock screen, even when the app is closed.
• Sunrise and sunset are worked out on the phone from your approximate location (or Amritsar if you prefer not to share it).

TIMERS
• Sadhana & vrat timer: Aarti, Chalisa, Path and Havan presets, any custom duration, and "Vrat until sunset".
• Sadhana session timer: set a time for your japa or paath and Sadho rings when it is done, with the screen off or the phone locked.
• Paath stopwatch with laps.

CALENDAR WITH EVENT REMINDERS
• Mark auspicious, cautious or ordinary days with an icon, a label and notes.
• Reminders at one or several times, repeating daily, weekly, monthly, quarterly, half-yearly or yearly. They ring on time and survive a restart.
• A daily reminder for your sadhana.

JAPA AND PAATH COUNTER
• Count by tap, by rhythm, with the volume keys (Mala mode, even with the screen off), or by voice (Beta): train Sadho on your own mantra and it counts each recitation, fully offline.
• Targets by count (27, 108, 1008 or your own) or by time, with a completion ring, vibration and a choice of bell sounds.
• Mantra library with Om Namah Shivaya, Gayatri Mantra, Waheguru and Mool Mantar, plus your own mantras.
• Focus mode, sankalp (intention), and an optional quiet mode that silences other notifications during a session.

HOME
• Greeting, today at a glance (sunrise and sunset), your calendar cards, and paath & mantra plans with a daily streak.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• Your practice, calendar, voice training and location stay on this phone.
• Voice counting keeps only feature numbers, never your audio.
• Back up to a file you choose, and restore it any time.

Nine languages: English, Hindi, Punjabi, Marathi, Bengali, Gujarati, Tamil, Telugu and Kannada. Light and dark themes, five colour palettes and a high-contrast option.
```
